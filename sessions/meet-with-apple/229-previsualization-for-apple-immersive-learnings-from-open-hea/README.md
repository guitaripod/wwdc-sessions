# Previsualization for Apple Immersive: Learnings from “Open Hearts” by The Weeknd

**Event:** Meet with Apple · **Topic:** Spatial Computing · **Platforms:** visionOS · **Published:** 2025-12-05 · **Session:** [meet-with-apple-229](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/meet-with-apple/229)

See how immersive previsualization elevates planning for Apple Immersive Video productions. This session demonstrates how CG tools helped test framing, movement, motion comfort, and VFX concepts in advance, which enabled alignment across teams and supported confident creative decisions for The Weeknd’s “Open Hearts" music video. This session was originally presented as part of the Meet with Apple activity “Create immersive media experiences for visionOS - Day 2.” Watch the full video for more insights and related sessions.
