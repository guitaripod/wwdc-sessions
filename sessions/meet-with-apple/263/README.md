# Get ready for the Swift Student Challenge

**Event:** Meet with Apple · **Topic:** SwiftUI & UI Frameworks · **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS · **Published:** 2026-01-22 · **Session:** [meet-with-apple-263](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/meet-with-apple/263)

Get ready with this online session, where you’ll explore app development resources with an Apple expert, get inspiration from previous winners, and spark ideas for building your own app playground.
