**[4:49]** From an engineering perspective, Liquid Glass was easy to integrate, but it did surface a few subtle challenges that required some thoughtful iteration to give our team control over where the effect was applied. This approach worked well, but it also surfaced a few key lessons. Avoid nested glass effect modifiers. Applying the glass effect to both a parent and child views led to visual redundancy.
