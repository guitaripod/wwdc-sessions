**Event:** Meet with Apple · **Topic:** Design · **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, macOS · **Published:** 2025-12-18 · **Session:** [meet-with-apple-255](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/meet-with-apple/255)

Find out how Slack reimagined their app experience for the new design and Liquid Glass.
