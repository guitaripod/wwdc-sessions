# Boost your app’s speed and efficiency

**Event:** Meet with Apple · **Topic:** Developer Tools · **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS · **Session:** [meet-with-apple-284](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/meet-with-apple/284)

Learn how to maximize your app’s performance and efficiency with Apple tools and technologies. In this online event, you'll explore how to identify performance opportunities and use Xcode and Instruments to gather and analyze key diagnostics. You’ll discover how to derive actionable insights from your data, including using coding agents to assist in building optimizations. You’ll also have the chance to take part in a Q&A with Apple experts.
