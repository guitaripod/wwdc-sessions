**[3:55]** Views and view controllers automatically update their trait collections and provide reasonable fallbacks even when they are not part of the visible hierarchy. If a change to a tracked trait is detected, the system will ensure these methods are called again so your UI automatically updates accordingly. **[4:42]** This method allows you to set up a closure that is called when a specific trait in the receiving view changes. Use this to invalidate caches or update other data related to that view.
