# Challenge: Achievement Unlocked — Series Finale

**Event:** WWDC21 · **Published:** 2021-06-10 · **Session:** [wwdc2021-10577](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=g1c0adgk)

Whether your game is divided into chapters, levels, or challenges, you can use achievements to help progress your narrative in unique and interesting ways. In this challenge — our series finale — we invite you to create a series of achievements that tell a story and encourage people to complete a set of tasks.
