Finally, a Live Activity allows quick actions and responses to displayed information. Live Activities are available on the inner display of iPhone Duo and outer, in StandBy mode, and CarPlay dashboard, in the Smart Stack of Apple Watch and iPad, and in the menu bar of your Mac,

On a Mac with Dynamic Island, Live Activities blend in seamlessly into the top edge of the device. On other devices and displays, they are shown in the menu bar. Live Activities are already present on the Mac, displayed by various system apps and activities, from alarms and timers, to currently playing music and videos, to receiving and handling FaceTime and phone calls.
