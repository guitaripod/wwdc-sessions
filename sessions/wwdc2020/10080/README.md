# Optimize your Shortcuts

**Event:** WWDC20 · **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, watchOS · **Published:** 2020-06-22 · **Session:** [wwdc2020-10080](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=g58zvm7o)

Help people get better sleep — and get more done — with Wind Down, Automations, and the Shortcuts app, now on watchOS. We’ll show you how to feature your intents in the Shortcuts app, integrate with Automation Suggestions, participate in Wind Down for the new Sleep experience, and build actions optimized for quick interactions on Apple Watch.
